## 1. Daayra

Yeh ek fully-automatic trading bot hai jo BankNifty, Nifty50 aur Sensex ke options par kaam karta hai. Bot teeno indices ko alag-alag dekhta hai aur har index ka apna score va faisla hota hai. Agar bot ko lagta hai ki index upar jayega, toh woh Call Option khareedta hai. Agar bot ko lagta hai ki index neeche jayega, toh woh Put Option khareedta hai. Shartein poori hone par bot broker ke manjoor raaste se khud order lagata hai.

Trade se nikalna aur Stop-Loss va Risk Limit sambhalna bhi bot ka hi hissa hai. Saath mein ek alag live Dashboard (website) hoga. Malik wahin se Mode Chunav karta hai aur bot ko Start Control ya Stop Control se chalu ya band karta hai (dekho: Dashboard). Dashboard dikhata bhi hai ki bot kya kar raha hai. Trade ka faisla Dashboard nahi, bot leta hai. Bot Upstox broker ke saath chalega, yeh user ne kaha hai. Bot kya nahi karega, uski poori baat agle paragraph mein ek hi jagah likhi hai.

Agar sell-side spread ya auto-hedging (jaise Bull Call Spread) ho, toh bot woh nahi karega. Wajah: yeh sell-side kaam hai aur bot ka daayra sirf option khareedne tak hai. Bechna sirf khareede hue option ko band karne ke liye hota hai. Bot raat bhar koi position nahi rakhega aur apne niyam ya vazan khud nahi badlega. Bot insaan se confirmation maangkar entry nahi karega `[TAKRAAV #1]`, bahari stocks nahi chhuega, aur chhoote trade ke peeche nahi bhaagega. Malik ka Start Control ya Stop Control dabana har entry ki confirmation nahi hai. `Target_Premium_Range` ab malik fix nahi karta, bot market dekh kar khud tay karta hai. Bot sirf us range ke premium khareedta hai. Bot batata hai ki aaj kitna paisa chahiye (Fund Request), malik utna paisa account mein daalkar Start Control dabata hai. Range ke bahar ka premium bot khud nahi kharidta, sirf Telegram par Range Alert deta hai (dekho: Alerts aur insaan ki bhoomika). Bot aapke balance ko percentage risk mein nahi bandhta, sirf dekhta hai ki balance mein kitne poore Lot ka paisa hai (dekho: Risk aur Lot ginti).

Nuksan ke baad badla lene ke liye niyam todna bhi bot kabhi nahi karega. Yeh file sabse pehle us AI ke liye hai jo isse PRD likhega. Doosra padhne wala is bot ka malik hai. Bot sirf apne account ke liye hai ya doosron ke liye bhi, yeh tay nahi hai (dekho: Abhi tay nahi hua).

## 2. Naam-Suchi

- **Option Chain:** Har index ke alag-alag strike aur unke Call va Put premium ki live suchi.
- **Call Option:** Woh contract jo index ke upar jaane ki ummeed par khareeda jaata hai.
- **Put Option:** Woh contract jo index ke neeche jaane ki ummeed par khareeda jaata hai.
- **Spot Price:** Index ka chal raha asli live bazar bhav.
- **Futures:** Index ka current-month vaayda contract jismein asli trading volume hota hai.
- **Premium:** Option contract ko khareedne ya bechne ka bhav.
- **Strike:** Option Chain ka woh tay index level jis par Call ya Put contract chuna jaata hai.
- **In-The-Money (ITM):** Woh strike jismein pehle se intrinsic value hoti hai aur jismein theta kam galta hai.
- **Lot:** Exchange dwara tay ki gayi ek contract ki nishchit quantity.
- **Stop-Loss:** Trade ko bade nuksan se bachane ke liye bot ke andar tay kiya gaya nikalne ka bhav.
- **Trailing Stop-Loss:** Munafa badhne par Stop-Loss ko peeche-peeche munafe ki disha mein khiskane ka niyam.
- **Time-Stop:** Tay samay tak premium mein chaal na aane par trade se bahar nikalne ka niyam.
- **Broker Stop:** Entry ke turant baad broker ke paas rakha gaya chhoot (buffer) wala aakhri Stop-Loss Limit Order.
- **Risk Limit:** Paise aur nuksan ki upari hadd (ek din ya ek hafte ke liye).
- **Daily Profit Lock:** Din mein acchi kamai hone par munafe ka bada hissa surakshit lock karne ka niyam (Munafa Bachav Rule).
- **Limit Order:** Entry ke waqt chalte ask bhav ke upar tay slippage buffer tak lagaya jaane wala adhikatam rate wala order.
- **Aggressive Limit Order:** Exit ke waqt turant bahar nikalne ke liye chalte bid bhav se thoda neeche lagaya jaane wala Limit Order.
- **Order Slicing:** Bade Lot wale order ko exchange freeze limit ya Market Depth ke hisaab se chhote tukdon mein baantna.
- **Slippage:** Signal aane wale bhav aur order ke asal fill hone wale bhav ke beech ka farak.
- **Market Depth:** Broker ke paas dikhne wale saamne ke top kharidne (bid) aur bechne (ask) wale pending orders ke levels.
- **Pehredaar:** Bot se alag chalne wala chhota hissa (watchdog) jo bot ki dhadkan dekhta hai aur atakne par trade band karta hai.
- **Dashboard:** Bot ke dimaag, checks, trades aur kundali ko live dikhane wali alag website. Isse malik Mode Chunav, Start aur Stop karta hai. Yeh khud trade ka faisla nahi leta.
- **Emergency Pause:** Dashboard ka woh suraksha button jo sirf naye entry rokta hai aur khule trades ko chalne deta hai.
- **Kill-Switch:** Dashboard ka woh suraksha button jo sab kuch turant rokta hai, pending orders cancel karta hai aur khule trades band karta hai.
- **Paper Mode:** Asli paise ke bina kaagaz par asli Market Depth, Slippage aur kharchon ke saath bot ko parakhne ka mode.
- **Real Money Mode:** Woh mode jismein bot broker account mein asli paise se asli order lagata hai.
- **Mode Chunav:** Start se pehle Dashboard par Paper Mode ya Real Money Mode mein se ek chunna. Malik dono mein se koi bhi chun sakta hai.
- **Start Control:** Dashboard ka woh button jo chune hue mode mein bot ko chalu karta hai.
- **Target Premium Range:** Premium ka woh daayra jo bot market dekh kar khud tay karta hai (user ka udaharan: 200 se 300 rupaye). Bot sirf isi range ke premium khareedta hai.
- **Fund Request:** Bot ka Telegram aur Dashboard par bheja sandesh jismein bot batata hai ki aaj account mein kitna paisa chahiye.
- **Range Alert:** Telegram ka woh alert jo tab aata hai jab koi "tagada" premium Target Premium Range ke bahar ho. "Tagada" ka maapdand tay nahi hua.
- **Telegram:** Phone par chhota alert bhejne ka madhyam. Isse sirf khabar milti hai.
- **Stop Control:** Dashboard ka woh button jo bot ko band karta hai. Iske baad khule trades ka kya hoga, yeh tay nahi hua.
- **Upstox:** Woh broker jiske account se bot judega (user ne bataya). Bot aur Pehredaar dono Upstox ke alag-alag app se judte hain.
- **Din ka Mausam:** Bazaar ke haal ka darja jo teen mein se ek hota hai: Trending, Sideways, ya Ulta-Palat.
- **No-Trade Day:** Sideways mausam wala din jab bot naye option khareedna band rakhta hai.
- **Momentum Zone:** Subah ka pehla trading samay jab bot aggressive hokar breakout pakadta hai.
- **Theta Trap Zone:** Dopahar ka woh samay jab premium galte hain, isliye bot naye entry nahi leta aur sirf khule trades sambhalta hai.
- **Gamma Blast Zone:** Dopahar ke baad ka tez momentum pakadne wala samay; expiry din ismein agli expiry ka strike khareeda jaata hai `[TAKRAAV #5]`.
- **Theta (Theta Decay):** Samay beetne ke saath option ke premium ke apne aap galne ki raftaar.
- **Delta:** Index ke ek point chalne par option ka premium kitna badalta hai, uska naap.
- **Gamma Explosion:** Expiry ya bade move mein option ke premium ka dugni raftaar se bhaagna.
- **Implied Volatility (IV):** Option ka premium kitna phoola hua ya mehenga hai, uska naap.
- **Realized Volatility:** Index ke Spot Price ki haal ki asli chaal ki raftaar.
- **IV Crush:** Badi khabar ya event ke turant baad phoo-le hue IV aur premium ka achanak gir jaana.
- **Implied Volatility Percentile (IVP):** Pichle ek saal ke IV record ke mukable aaj ka IV kitne percent dino se upar hai.
- **Shock Mode:** Teeno indices ke ek saath tezi se hilne aur India VIX ke achanak uchhalne par lagne wala suraksha lock.
- **GIFT Nifty:** Subah bazaar khulne se pehle raat ke global sanket dene wala index indicator.
- **ADX:** Trend ki taakat naapne wala chart meter.
- **VWAP:** Din bhar ke volume ke hisaab se nikala gaya ausat bhav.
- **ATR:** Haal ke samay mein bhav ke ausat utaar-chadhav ki doori.
- **EMA:** Haal ke bhav ko zyada vazan dene wali moving average line.
- **RSI:** Bhav ki tezi ya mandi ki taakat batane wala meter, aur bhav ke ulte chalne par dikhne wala RSI divergence.
- **Bollinger Bands aur Keltner Channel:** Bhav ke utaar-chadhav ki upari aur nichli pattiyan, jinke sikudne ko Squeeze kehte hain.
- **Squeeze:** Bollinger Bands aur Keltner Channel ka bahut zyada sikud jaana, jo batata hai ki dabi chaal phat sakti hai.
- **Fake Spike:** Option Chain mein aas-paas ke strikes ke bina sirf ek akele strike ke premium mein aaya nakli uchhal.
- **Iceberg Order:** Kisi bade khiladi dwara apne bade order ko chhote hisson mein chhupa kar lagatar khareedne ka andaza.
- **Chase Guard:** Pehle se bahut bhaag chuke premium ko upar khareedne (Late Entry) se rokne wala suraksha check.
- **Order Block:** Chart par bade khiladiyon ki bhaari khareed-bech ka nishaan dikhane wali candle ka andaza.
- **BOS (Break of Structure):** Chart par pichle swing level ka tootna jo batata hai ki chal raha trend jaari rahega.
- **CHoCH (Change of Character):** Chart ke structure mein pehla badlaav jo batata hai ki trend palat sakta hai.
- **Fair Value Gap (FVG):** Tez chaal mein bani candles ke beech ka woh khaali hissa jahan bhav wapas aa sakta hai.
- **Open Interest (OI):** Kisi strike par abhi tak khule hue kul contracts ki sankhya.
- **OI Deewar:** Sabse zyada Call OI wala strike (upar ki rukaawat) aur sabse zyada Put OI wala strike (neeche ka sahara).
- **Short-Covering (Unwinding):** Option bechne walon ka dar kar apni khuli positions kaatna, jisse us strike par OI tezi se ghat-ta hai.
- **Max Pain:** Option Chain ka woh strike jahan expiry par option khareedne walon ko sabse zyada nuksan hota hai.
- **Gamma Exposure (GEX):** Option Chain mein dealers kis bhav par chaal ko dheema aur kis bhav par tez karenge, uska andaza.
- **Positive Gamma Day:** Jab GEX ke hisaab se bazaar ke ek daayre (range) mein bandhe rehne ki ummeed ho.
- **Negative Gamma Day:** Jab GEX ke hisaab se bazaar mein tez trend ya breakout aane ki ummeed ho.
- **Gamma Flip:** Woh Spot Price level jahan kul GEX positive se negative (ya ulta) mein badalta hai.
- **Pre-Breakout Alert:** Shant index mein bina khabar ke kisi strike ka premium aur OI achanak badhne ki aahat.
- **SUSPICIOUS:** Manipulation check mein gadbad milne par lagne wala flag jo us index mein naya trade block karta hai.
- **Put-Call Ratio (PCR):** Put aur Call ke OI ya volume ka anupaat jo bazaar ka kul mood dikhata hai.
- **Breadth (Advance/Decline):** Index ke andar ke kul stocks mein se kitne upar hain aur kitne neeche, unke vazan ke saath.
- **Heavyweight Stocks:** Index mein sabse zyada vazan rakhne wale bade stocks (jaise HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Reliance, Infosys).
- **India VIX:** Bazaar mein dar ka meter, aur uska Term-Structure (is expiry aur agli expiry ke IV ka anupaat).
- **USD/INR, Crude Oil aur Bond Yield:** Dollar-rupaye ka bhav, kacche tel ka bhav, aur Bharat ke sarkari bond par milne wala byaaj.
- **Participant-Wise Data:** Exchange dwara har shaam diya jaane wala FII, DII, Pro aur Client ki kul positions ka record.
- **Heartbeat Timestamp:** Har naye aane wale tick data ke samay ki chhap, jisse pata chalta hai ki data kitna taza hai.
- **Gate:** Aisa check jo RED hone par score kuch bhi ho, trade skip kar deta hai.
- **Score:** GREEN checks ke points ka jod, jo trade ke liye minimum se upar hona chahiye.
- **Confidence:** Kamzor sthiti mein trade ke liye zaroori minimum score ko upar badhane ka niyam.
- **Breakout:** Bhav ka kisi tay range ya level ko tod kar bahar nikalna.
- **Trap:** Nakli breakout jo Stop-Loss udane ke baad bhav ko wapas range mein ghusa deta hai.
- **Wick:** Candle ke upar ya neeche ki lambi patli lakeer jo us level se bhav ke wapas dhakelne ko dikhati hai.
- **Breakeven:** Woh bhav jahan kharcha nikaal kar trade na munafa deta hai na nuksan.
- **Cooldown:** Trade band hone ke baad naye entry se pehle ruke rehne ka tay samay.
- **Drawdown:** Account ki sabse oonchi kamai se neeche ki girawat.
- **Freeze Limit:** Exchange ki woh adhikatam quantity jo ek order mein lag sakti hai.
- **Backtest:** Purane bazaar data par niyam chala kar dekhna ki woh tab kaise chalte.
- **Expiry:** Option contract ke khatam hone ka din.
- **Whipsaw:** Bhav ka jaldi-jaldi idhar-udhar palatna, jisse Stop-Loss baar-baar lagte hain.
- **Rollover:** Stocks ke F&O contracts ka purani expiry se agli expiry mein badla jaana.
- **Bid aur Ask:** Bid khareedne walon ka sabse oonchi kimat. Ask bechne walon ki sabse kam kimat.
- **Circuit-limit:** Exchange ki woh hadd jisse zyada bhav ek din mein nahi chal sakta.
- **Trading Halt:** Exchange dwara trading ko rok diya jaana.
- **Revenge Trading:** Nuksan ke baad badla lene ke liye jaldbaazi mein trade lena.
- **Tick Size:** Option ke bhav ka sabse chhota kadam, jis par broker order leta hai.
- **Static IP (Primary aur Secondary):** Broker ke paas darj tay internet pate, jahan se hi order bhejne ki ijaazat hoti hai (bot ke liye Primary aur Pehredaar ke liye Secondary).
- **Algo-ID:** Broker ki di hui pehchaan jo bot ke order par lagti hai, taaki pata chale yeh algo order hai.
- **Algo Name:** Woh naam jo bot har order ke saath Upstox ko bhejta hai, taaki pata chale yeh algo order hai.
- **MIS aur NRML:** MIS woh order jo usi din band karna hota hai. NRML woh order jo agle din tak rakha ja sakta hai.
- **Prastaavit:** Woh faisla ya number jo malik ne nahi diya aur 9 Nov 2026 ko khaali jagah bharne ke liye sujhaya gaya. Yeh Andaza se bhi kam pakka hai. Malik ki pushti tak ise badla ja sakta hai.
- **NA (Lagu Nahi):** Check ka teesra haal, jab data na mile ya check us din lagu na ho. Gate ka NA RED maana jaata hai.
- **Shadow Mode:** Check chalta hai aur Dashboard par dikhta hai, par score, Gate ya Confidence par asar nahi daalta. Jaanch poori hone tak check isi mein rehta hai.
- **Stage:** Test ka ek charan: Paper Stage, Stage2 (`Test_Stage2_Live_Lot`) aur Poora Lot Stage.
- **Order Tag:** Bot ke har order par laga alag naam-chhap, jisse bot ke order pehchane jaate hain.
- **Signal ID:** Ek signal ki pehchaan: index, strike, Call ya Put aur woh candle jis par signal bana.
- **Intent Log:** Order bhejne se pehle bot ka likha irada (Signal ID, Order Tag, quantity, bhav). Dobara chalu hone par bot ise padhta hai.
- **Net Quantity Guard:** Woh niyam ki kisi bhi option ki net quantity kabhi zero se neeche (net short) na jaaye.
- **Order Baari:** Broker ko jaane wale kaamon ki line. Pehle Broker Stop aur exit, phir entry, phir record milaan, phir data ke request.
- **Instrument Master:** Roz subah broker se aane wali suchi jismein har contract ka Lot, freeze limit, Tick Size aur expiry ka din hota hai.
- **MPP Order (Protected Market):** Broker ka woh order jo market jaisa turant bhaagta hai par ek tay seema ke andar rehta hai.
- **Pehredaar ka Exit Hissa:** Pehredaar ka woh chhota hissa jo bot ke bina bhi broker par Broker Stop cancel karke trade band kar sakta hai.
- **Disha:** Index ki chaal ki ek hi paribhasha: UP, DOWN ya FLAT, `Disha_Paribhasha` ke hisaab se.
- **Foreign Position (Haath ka Position):** Malik ne broker app se haath se jo trade liya. Usmein bot ka Order Tag nahi hota.

## 3. Bot ka ek poora din

Bot ka process roz subah apne aap chalu ho jaata hai aur sirf dekhta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Trade lene ki ijaazat tabhi milti hai jab malik Dashboard par Mode Chunav karke Start Control dabata hai (dekho: Dashboard). Start Control us din ki poori ijaazat hai, har entry ki confirmation nahi. Wajah: user ne kaha bot website se chalu hoga aur entry bot khud karega.

Subah ki taiyari bot khud jaanchta hai. Woh broker ka naya login, order bhejne ka rasta aur Static IP, live data, Instrument Master, free paisa aur margin dekhta hai. Woh pichle din ki bachi position, Foreign Position, economic calendar ki umar aur `IVP_Lookback_Din` ka IV record bhi jaanchta hai. Woh bade index stocks mein dividend ya khaas badlaav aur apni ghadi ka exchange ki ghadi se farak (`Ghadi_Skew_Hadd`) bhi dekhta hai. Wajah: dividend se Futures bhav mein aane wale farak ko bot galat signal na samajh le.

Agar subah ki taiyari mein kuch bhi kharab ho, toh bot aaj naye entry nahi leta. Dashboard par "Taiyari Fail" aur wajah ki suchi dikhti hai aur malik ko turant phone alert jaata hai. Malik Start Control dobara dabakar `Taiyari_Dobara_Koshish_Ginti` tak koshish kar sakta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: adhoori taiyari ke saath asli paise ka jokhim nahi liya ja sakta.

Upstox ka login token agle din subah 3:30 baje tak hi chalta hai. Isliye bot aur Pehredaar roz subah naya login karte hain `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Lambe samay wala token sirf padhne ke kaam ka hai, usse order nahi lagte. Wajah: purane token par bhrosa karke order lagane ki koshish na ho.

Economic calendar aur khaas dinon ki suchi malik ek alag file mein rakhta hai aur har hafte badalta hai. Agar aaj khaas din ho (Budget, Muhurat ya exchange ka special session) ya aaj ka din suchi mein na mile, toh bot trade nahi leta. Agar suchi `Calendar_Purana_Hadd` se purani ho, toh bhi bot trade nahi leta `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bharosemand calendar srot abhi nahi mila (dekho: Tayyar Code (Repo)) aur aise din normal niyamon se alag chalte hain.

Start se pehle bot pichli closing ke bhav se anumaan lagata hai ki har index mein 1 Lot ka kharcha kitna hoga. Woh ismein `Balance_Extra_Buffer` jodkar Fund Request Telegram aur Dashboard par bhejta hai. Malik paisa daalkar Start Control dabata hai. Agar free paisa kisi bhi index ke 1 Lot aur `Balance_Extra_Buffer` ke liye kam ho, toh Start Control band rehta hai aur "Paisa Kam" dikhta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: pre-open mein options ka bhav nahi chalta, isliye Fund Request sirf anumaan hai. Start se pehle bot sirf dekhta hai aur trade nahi leta.

Asli `Target_Premium_Range` bot har index ke liye alag, 9:15 ke baad ke asli bhav aur asli free paisa se `Range_Tay_Karne_Ka_Tarika` ke hisaab se tay karta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Woh range har `Mausam_Dobara_Jaanch_Samay` par aur expiry shift par agli expiry ke bhav se dobara tay hoti hai. Agar kisi index mein Delta band ka koi bhi strike range mein na aaye, toh woh index us waqt tradable nahi maana jaata aur Dashboard par "Range Mein Strike Nahi" dikhta hai. Wajah: ek hi rupaye ki range teeno index ke Lot aur Delta par nahi baithti.

Malik `Fund_Intezaar_Samay` tak paisa daale. Agar paisa der se aaye, toh bot nayi Fund Request nahi bhejta aur jo free paisa hai usmein sabse achha premium leta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Agar malik zyada paisa daale, toh Lot asli free paisa se tay hote hain, Fund Request se nahi. Dopahar mein bot doosri Fund Request nahi bhejta; naya paisa agli range ginti par dikhta hai. Wajah: Lot ginti hamesha asli free paisa par tiki hai.

Subah hi Pehredaar bhi alag chalu ho jaata hai (dekho: Pehredaar aur Suraksha). Market khulne ke baad shuru ke `Shuruaati_Dekhne_Ka_Samay` tak bot sirf dekhta hai aur trade nahi leta. Wajah: kam data par akela ADX bharose ka nahi hota aur sideways bazaar dushman hai. Bot ADX ko pichle din ki candles ke saath jodkar chalata hai (`ADX_Avadhi_Aur_Timeframe`) aur candles apne tick se banata hai, 9:15 se gin kar `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: subah ADX ko garam hone ke liye candles kam na pad jaayein.

Mausam tay karne ke liye woh pichle din ki range, closing aur aaj ka opening gap bhi dekhta hai. Woh raat ke global sanket, GIFT Nifty aur pichli shaam ka Participant-Wise Data bhi milata hai (dekho: Tayyar Code (Repo)). Agar market `Bada_Gap_Hadd` se khule aur pichli range se door ho, toh bot nayi range ka intezaar karta hai. Wajah: bade gap ke baad aksar bazaar thak kar sideways ho jaata hai.

Din ka Mausam teen mein se ek hota hai: Trending, Sideways ya Ulta-Palat. Agar ADX `ADX_Trending_Hadd` ya usse upar ho, toh mausam Trending hai. Agar ADX `ADX_Sideways_Hadd` ya usse neeche ho, toh mausam Sideways hai. Agar ADX in dono ke beech ho aur `Ulta_Palat_Pehchaan_Paimana` poora ho, toh mausam Ulta-Palat hai; warna Sideways maana jaata hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: beech ka ADX saaf trend nahi hai.

Agar Din ka Mausam Trending ho, toh bot normal breakout niyam se chalta hai. Wajah: ek disha ke trend mein breakout sabse achha kaam karta hai. Agar Din ka Mausam Sideways ho, toh bot No-Trade Day maan kar khareedna band rakhta hai. Galat Sideways tay hone se din ka mauka chhoot sakta hai; yeh jaanboojh kar maana gaya nuksan hai aur Report mein "Sideways Din" tag ke saath baad mein dekha jaata hai. Wajah: sideways bazaar mein Theta Decay premium ko gala deta hai.

Agar Din ka Mausam Ulta-Palat ho, toh bot sirf trap wale ulte entry leta hai. Ulta-Palat mein Lot `Ulta_Palat_Aur_Drawdown_Lot_Cut` hote hain (dekho: Risk aur Lot ginti), reversal checks lagte hain aur Time-Stop chhota hota hai. Wajah: aise din breakout phans jaate hain aur palat-na bhi jaldi hota hai.

Bot mausam ko `Mausam_Dobara_Jaanch_Samay` par dobara tay karta hai. Agar naya mausam `Mausam_Tikav_Samay` tak tike, tabhi bot mausam badalta hai. Zone ki seema par bhi naya mausam tabhi maana jaata hai jab woh `Mausam_Tikav_Samay` tak tike `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: chhote jhatke se bot idhar-udhar na hile.

Bot trading din ko teen hisson mein baantta hai, aur teeno ki seema ek tay samay hai, "lagbhag" nahi `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Agar samay `Momentum_Zone_Samay` mein ho, toh Momentum Zone mein bot aggressive hokar breakout pakadta hai. Wajah: subah sabse achha momentum milta hai. Agar samay `Theta_Trap_Zone_Samay` mein ho, toh Theta Trap Zone mein naya entry band rehta hai. Wajah: dopahar mein premium Theta Decay se galte hain, isliye sirf khule trades sambhale jaate hain.

Agar samay `Gamma_Blast_Zone_Samay` mein ho, toh Gamma Blast Zone mein bot phir tez momentum pakadta hai. Expiry ke din ke shift ke liye dekho: Expiry ke din ke niyam. Wajah: dopahar ke baad bazaar mein dobara tez chaal aati hai.

Agar economic calendar mein badi khabar ho, toh bot `Event_Lock_Pehle_Baad_Samay` tak naye entry lock karta hai. Khule trade ko woh event se `Event_Exit_Pehle_Samay` pehle band kar deta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: badi khabar par premium achanak ulta ja sakta hai aur IV Crush bhi ho sakta hai.

Event ke baad bhi bot rate ka farak aur IV thanda hone (`IV_Thanda_Paimana`) tak rukta hai. Agar teeno indices ek saath `Shock_Mode_Move_Aur_VIX_Hadd` se hilein, toh bot Shock Mode lagata hai. Shock Mode mein naye entry rukte hain aur khule trades turant band kiye jaate hain; Shock Mode `Shock_Mode_Khatam_Paimana` poora hone par hatta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bina bataye aaye achanak jhatke se paisa bachana zaroori hai.

Sham ko `Aakhri_Entry_Samay` ke baad bot naya entry nahi leta (dekho: Entry). Phir `Auto_Square_Off_Samay` ke andar saare khule trades apne aap band ho jaate hain (dekho: Exit). Agar usse pehle hi order book patli ho jaaye (`Liquidity_Filter` RED), toh bot khule trade jaldi nikaal deta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: aakhri minuton mein bid gayab ho sakte hain. Din ke ant mein bot apni roz ki report banata hai aur IV record va jama-nikasi ka khata bhi badhata hai (dekho: Report, Record aur Niyam badalna). Shaam ko exchange se Participant-Wise Data aata hai jo agle din ka context banta hai (dekho: Tayyar Code (Repo)). Raat ko bot ke paas koi khuli position nahi rehti.

## 4. Expiry ke din ke niyam

Har index ki expiry ka din bot roz Instrument Master ki contract suchi se leta hai, weekday ginkar nahi (dekho: Tarikh wali baatein) `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Chhutti hone par expiry pichle trading din par aa jaati hai, aur yeh bhi suchi se hi pata chalta hai. Bot subah hi har index ke liye alag expiry flag lagata hai. Wajah: kuch checks aur theta bachav ke niyam sirf expiry wale din chalte hain.

Agar aaj expiry ho aur subah Momentum Zone ho, toh bot mazboot tezi mein hi current expiry chunta hai. Us waqt woh `Expiry_Subah_Delta_Range` wala In-The-Money strike chunta hai. Wajah: thode andar wale strike mein subah Theta Decay kam galta hai.

Agar expiry ki subah current expiry ka trade liya ho, toh woh `Expiry_Shift_Aur_Exit_Samay` tak nikaal diya jaata hai. Stop-Loss tight karke trade rakhne ka rasta nahi hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: `Expiry_Shift_Aur_Exit_Samay` ke baad current expiry mein theta bahut tez ho jaata hai.

Agar aaj expiry ho aur `Expiry_Shift_Aur_Exit_Samay` baj jaaye, toh bot current expiry ka strike nahi khareedta. Woh apne aap agli expiry ke strike par shift ho jaata hai. Zone ka naam Gamma Blast Zone hi rehta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Current expiry ki Gamma Explosion tezi ko woh sirf ek signal ki tarah dekhta hai. Wajah: dopahar ke tez Theta Decay se bachna zaroori hai. Nifty50 aur Sensex mein agli expiry agle hafte ki aur BankNifty mein agle mahine ki hoti hai.

Shift ke second par pehle khule trade ka exit hota hai, phir naya entry, kyunki Order Baari mein exit pehle aata hai. Shift ke baad agli expiry ke liye `Target_Premium_Range` aur Delta dobara dekhe jaate hain. Agar agli expiry ka koi strike Range aur Delta dono mein na aaye, toh us index mein trade nahi hota. Wajah: agli expiry ka bhav same Delta par alag hota hai.

BankNifty ki sirf mahine ki expiry hoti hai. Isliye BankNifty ka `Expiry_Shift_Aur_Exit_Samay` wala shift sirf us mahine ki expiry ke din lagta hai. Baaki dino BankNifty mahine ke contract mein chalta hai aur usmein Delta aur liquidity checks aur dhyan se lagte hain `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: BankNifty mein weekly expiry nahi hai.

Agar agli expiry se bhi zyada door ka contract ho, toh bot use nahi chunta. Wajah: bahut door wale options index ke saath theek se nahi chalte. Agar agli expiry ka koi strike saare checks pass na kare, toh bot trade nahi leta. Wajah: door ke contract mein kam liquidity hone par zabardasti trade nahi lena chahiye.

`Manipulation_Settlement_Check` current expiry ke settlement ki jaanch karta hai. Agar woh SUSPICIOUS de, toh us index mein agli expiry ka naya trade bhi nahi hota `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: SUSPICIOUS flag us index ke naye trade ko block karta hai, chahe contract koi bhi ho.

Jis din Nifty50, BankNifty aur stocks ke contracts ek saath expire hon (Instrument Master se pata chalta hai; aam taur par mahine ka aakhri Mangalwar, dekho: Tarikh wali baatein), bot Nifty50 aur BankNifty ko ek hi bade daon ki tarah ginta hai. ## 5. Pehredaar aur Suraksha

Bot regulator aur exchange ke retail algo niyam ke andar kaam karta hai (dekho: Tarikh wali baatein). Woh broker ke manjoor raaste se hi judta hai (dekho: Tayyar Code (Repo)). Bot sirf apni darj Static IP se, apne Algo Name ke saath order bhejta hai aur roz naya login karta hai. Woh har second ke kaamon ko `Order_Prati_Second_Seema` ke andar Order Baari se bhejta hai. Wajah: exchange ke niyam kabhi na tootein; modify aur cancel bhi ginti mein maane jaate hain.

Bot ke saath ek chhota alag Pehredaar chalta hai (dekho: Tayyar Code (Repo)). Woh alag machine par, apne alag Upstox app, alag token aur Secondary Static IP ke saath chalta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Pehredaar har `Pehredaar_Dhadkan_Samay` mein bot ki dhadkan dekhta hai. Agar `Pehredaar_Dhadkan_Chhoot_Ginti` dhadkan lagatar chhoot jaayein, toh Pehredaar ka Exit Hissa bot ke khule trade ko surakshit band karta hai. Wajah: Pehredaar alag chalne ke kaaran bot ke saath kharab nahi hota.

Upstox mein ek app ke saath ek hi token chalta hai. Isliye Pehredaar ka apna alag app hai, taaki uska login bot ka login na tode `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bot ke chalte Pehredaar ke login se bot ka token band na ho. Dashboard se Stop Control dabane par Dashboard ek "Malik ne roka" nishan lagata hai, jise Pehredaar padhta hai. Is nishan ke saath bot ke ruk jaane ko Pehredaar crash nahi maanta; woh sirf dekhta hai ki koi khula trade bina Broker Stop ke na bache. Wajah: malik ke band karne par Pehredaar trade na kaate.

Bot koi bhi order bhejne se pehle Intent Log mein apna irada likhta hai. Har order par Order Tag lagta hai aur har signal ki ek Signal ID hoti hai. Agar order ka jawab na aaye, toh bot dobara bhejne se pehle Order Tag se broker se poochta hai ki order hai ya nahi. Ek Signal ID par dobara order nahi lagta jab tak `Signal_Dobara_Order_Roak_Samay` na beete `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: jawab na aane par dobara bheja order dohra trade bana sakta hai.

Entry fill hone ke `Entry_Ke_Baad_Broker_Stop_Samay` ke andar Broker Stop lag jaana chahiye. Agar tab tak na lage, toh bot trade turant band karta hai. Broker Stop ki quantity hamesha broker ki confirm ki hui fill quantity ke barabar rehti hai aur har aadhe fill ya partial exit par badalti hai. Agar fill 1 Lot se kam ho, toh bot use turant band karta hai aur bacha order cancel karta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bina Stop ka trade khula nahi rehna chahiye.

Broker Stop bot ke apne Stop-Loss se `Broker_Stop_Doori_Aur_Buffer` door, chhoot wale limit bhav par lagta hai (dekho: Tarikh wali baatein). Wajah: roz ka Stop-Loss bot khud sambhale aur Broker Stop sirf bot rukne par chale. Agar bot apna Stop-Loss aage khiskata hai, toh Broker Stop bhi saath mein khisakta hai. Har khiskaav ka hukm broker se confirm hona chahiye. Agar hukm do baar reject ho, toh bot trade turant band karta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: purana, door wala Broker Stop bina khabar ke trade ko zyada nuksan de sakta hai.

Bot ka har bhav (Limit Order, Broker Stop, Trailing Stop-Loss) ek hi jagah se Tick Size par round hota hai aur option ke Circuit-limit ke andar rakha jaata hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Kharidne wale bhav upar aur bechne wale bhav neeche round hote hain. Wajah: Tick Size ya Circuit-limit ke bahar ka bhav broker reject kar deta hai.

Net Quantity Guard hamesha lagu rehta hai. Trade band karte waqt bot pehle Broker Stop cancel karta hai aur broker ke cancel ka confirm `Broker_Stop_Cancel_Intezaar_Samay` tak dekhta hai. Cancel confirm hote hi woh exit order lagata hai. Agar broker bole ki Broker Stop pehle hi fill ho chuka, toh trade band maana jaata hai aur exit order nahi lagta. Agar exit order fill na ho, toh bot Broker Stop turant dobara lagata hai. Exit ke baad bot broker se net quantity padhta hai; agar woh zero se neeche ho, toh turant barabar quantity khareed kar band karta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: exit aur Broker Stop dono fill hone par account net short na ho jaaye.

Tez jhatke mein bhav Broker Stop ki limit kood sakta hai. Agar order `Broker_Stop_Fill_Intezaar_Samay` mein fill na ho, toh bot ya Pehredaar ka Exit Hissa naye bhav par MPP Order se trade band karta hai. Agar broker ya exchange ruka ho (halt, circuit ya bid na ho), toh naye entry band rehte hain, Broker Stop khula rehta hai aur malik ko critical alert jaata hai. Khulte hi bot MPP Order se nikalta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: tez girawat mein order pending rehne se bada nuksan ho sakta hai.

Kill-Switch ka hukm Dashboard se seedha Pehredaar ke Exit Hissa ko jaata hai, bot ko nahi `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Woh pehle pending entry orders cancel karta hai. Phir har khule trade par upar wala exit kram chalata hai: Broker Stop cancel, confirm, phir exit. Woh kisi bhi Broker Stop ko aise cancel nahi karta jisse khula trade bina Stop ke rah jaaye. Kaam khatam hone par Dashboard par "ho gaya" ya "fail" dikhta hai; fail par critical alert jaata hai aur Broker Stop dobara lagta hai. Wajah: bot atka ho tab bhi Kill-Switch kaam kare.

Agar bot dobara chalu ho, toh uska pehla kaam broker se bot ke Order Tag wali khuli positions aur pending orders padhna hai. Agar koi khula trade bina Broker Stop mile, toh bot pehle use Broker Stop lagata hai; woh `Entry_Ke_Baad_Broker_Stop_Samay` mein na lage toh trade band karta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: crash ke beech ka trade bina bachav ke rah sakta hai.

Bot har `Broker_Record_Milaan_Samay` mein apne record ko broker ke asli record se milata hai. Agar bot ka trade haath se band hua ho ya record mein farak aaye, toh bot apna record sudhaarta hai aur `Record_Farak_Pause_Samay` ke liye naye entry rokta hai. Woh purana Broker Stop tabhi cancel karta hai jab broker us trade ki quantity zero dikhaye. Foreign Position ko bot na chhoota hai na band karta hai; woh use Dashboard par dikhata hai aur malik ko khabar deta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bot malik ke haath ke trade ko galti samajh kar kaat na de.

Agar khule trade ke dauran bhav ka data `Blind_Position_Hadd` se zyada purana ho, toh bot seedha broker se naya bhav mangta hai aur trade MPP Order se band karta hai. Agar woh bhi na mile, toh sirf Broker Stop par bharosa hota hai aur critical alert jaata hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: expiry jaise tez waqt mein andhe trade ko khula nahi chhodna chahiye.

Broker ke har kaam Order Baari se jaate hain. Order Baari broker ki per-second, per-minute aur per-hour seema ke andar rehti hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: `Expiry_Shift_Aur_Exit_Samay` par exit, naya Broker Stop aur entry ek hi second mein aa sakte hain.

Bot har `Order_Path_Jaanch_Samay` par order ke raste ki jaanch karta hai, aisa kaam jisme asli order nahi lagta. Agar Static IP ya login ka masla ho, toh naye entry band hote hain, critical alert jaata hai aur sirf Broker Stop bachta hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: bot zinda ho par har order reject ho raha ho, tab bhi khule trade ki suraksha bani rahe.

Alag-alag reject par alag kaam hota hai `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Paisa kam: aaj naye entry band aur alert. Price band ya Tick Size: bhav ek baar sudhaar kar dobara. Freeze quantity: strike chhodna. Static IP ya Algo Name ka masla: naye entry band, critical alert. Exchange halt ya market band: intezaar. Instrument nahi mila: Instrument Master dobara. Wajah: `Order_Reject_Ginti` sirf ginti hai, wajah nahi batati.

Broker ki chaabi, roz ka login-secret aur Telegram ki chaabi sirf trading machine ke surakshit store mein rehti hain. Woh kisi file, log ya Dashboard mein nahi aati aur Dashboard ke paas unka rasta nahi hota `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Wajah: Dashboard internet par khula hota hai aur chaabi leak hone se asli paise ka khatra hai. Paper Mode ka code order bhejne ka rasta hi nahi rakhta aur uski chaabi alag hoti hai.

## 14. Alerts aur insaan ki bhoomika

Wajah: user ne kaha Telegram par khali alert aur website par achhe se.

Alert ke teen darje hain `[PRASTAAVIT]`. Critical Alert: Pehredaar chale, Broker Stop na lage, Kill-Switch fail ho, khule trade ka bhav na mile, order ka raasta kharab ho ya broker va exchange ruke. Zaroori Alert: taiyari fail, order reject, bot pause, Risk Limit toote, Foreign Position mile aur roz subah ke login ki manzoori. ## 20. Tarikh wali baatein

Pehli baat (SEBI Retail Algo Niyam): Bot SEBI aur exchange ke retail algo niyam ke andar manjoor raaste se chalta hai. Yeh niyam 1 April 2026 se poori tarah lagu hain. Bot ek tay darj jagah se order bhejta hai, roz naya login karta hai aur algo pehchaan rakhta hai. Woh har second ke orders ki ginti ko `Order_Prati_Second_Seema` ke andar rakhta hai. Broker ke notice ke hisaab se Static IP ek ya do hote hain, aur har algo order par exchange ki di Algo-ID lagti hai. Roz ek baar 2FA zaroori hai aur lagatar chalne wala refresh-token login band hai. Regulator ki seema 10 orders prati second prati exchange prati client batayi gayi hai, jiske upar strategy darj karani padti hai. Kuch brokers ke hisaab se isme modify aur cancel bhi ginti mein aate hain. Market order MPP order mein badal jaata hai. Static IP hafte mein ek baar se zyada badalna mana hai (NSE ke standards ka saar). Kis tarikh tak sahi: 9 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: Fyers, Shoonya aur Aliceblue ke notice va blog; SEBI ya NSE ka circular seedha nahi padha).

Doosri baat (Expiry ke Weekday aur Settlement): Nifty50 ki weekly expiry har Mangalwar ko aur Sensex ki har Guruwar ko hoti hai. BankNifty ki expiry mahine mein ek baar mahine ke aakhri Mangalwar ko hoti hai (chhutti par pichle din). Aakhri Mangalwar ko Nifty50, BankNifty aur saare bade stocks ke F&O contracts ek saath expire hote hain. Expiry par options ka final settlement `Expiry_Settlement_Ausat_Samay` (`3:00 se 3:30 PM`) ke ausat Spot Price par hota hai. Sensex ki monthly expiry mahine ke aakhri Guruwar ko hoti hai. November 2026 mein Nifty50 ki weekly expiry 10 Nov se hatkar 9 Nov (Somvaar) ko aayi, aur NSE ke monthly contracts 24 Nov se hatkar 23 Nov ko expire honge. Ek page par Sensex ka din Mangalwar likha mila, baaki saare srot Guruwar kehte hain. Settlement wali baat dobara jaanchi nahi. Kis tarikh tak sahi: 9 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: algotest ka expiry calendar, share.market, Sahi aur onetradejournal; exchange ka circular seedha nahi padha).

Teesri baat (Lot, Freeze Limit aur Market-Stop): Ek Lot ki quantity aur freeze limit exchange samay-samay par tay karta hai. Exchange mein options par seedhe market-stop order band hain, isliye Broker Stop buffer wale limit bhav se lagta hai. Lot: Nifty50 mein 65, BankNifty mein 30 (January 2026 se) aur Sensex mein 20. Freeze Limit 2 Nov 2026 se Nifty50 mein 1,800 aur BankNifty mein 900 units hai (NSE circular NSE/FAOP/77189 ke hisaab se). 5 Oct 2026 wali badhi hui limit (Nifty50 3,510, BankNifty 1,440) ab hat chuki hai. Sensex ki freeze limit nahi mili. Market-stop wali baat dobara jaanchi nahi. Kis tarikh tak sahi: 9 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: TradingQnA aur TeamLease RegTech ke circular saar, Sahi va Upstox ke lot-size notice; NSE ka circular seedha nahi padha).

Chauthi baat (Khaas Trading Din): Budget ka din ya Muhurat trading jaise khaas dino ki tarikh har saal alag hoti hai. Aise din bot bina malik ki manzoori ke apne aap normal niyam se trade nahi leta `[TAKRAAV #1]`. Kis tarikh tak sahi: 7 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: user ki file).

Paanchvi baat (Jane Street Case): SEBI ne 3 July 2025 ke order mein 4,843.57 crore rupaye impound karne ko kaha tha. Case Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) mein chal raha hai. 21 Oct 2026 ko SAT ne Jane Street ki dastavez maangne wali arzi par order surakshit rakha, jo manipulation ka asli faisla nahi hai. Final faisla abhi nahi aaya hai aur Jane Street in aaropon ko nahi maanta. Bot kisi ko doshi nahi maanta balki isse Section 7 wale teen manipulation checks seekhta hai. Kis tarikh tak sahi: 9 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: Business Standard 21 Oct 2026 aur LiveLaw).

Chhathi baat (Upstox API ki Seemayein, Token aur Square-Off): Upstox API mein standard order API ki seema 10 request prati second, 500 prati minute aur 2,000 prati 30 minute hai (multi-order API 4 prati second). Ek din mein ek order zyada se zyada 25 baar modify ho sakta hai. Upstox ka access token har din agle din subah 3:30 AM par khatam ho jaata hai aur koi refresh token nahi hota. Ek app ke liye naya token banate hi purana token band ho jaata hai, par ek account mein kai apps banaye ja sakte hain. Lambe samay wala extended token sirf padhne (read-only) ke kaam aata hai, usse order nahi lagte. Har order ke headers mein Algo Name bhejna hota hai. Upstox ke Intraday (MIS) auto square-off ke samay par do alag baatein mili: ek help page par 3:20 PM aur naye update page par Equity F&O ke liye 3:25 PM `[Sawal 114]`. Kis tarikh tak sahi: 9 Nov 2026 (kisne batayi: Upstox developer documentation, Upstox community forum aur Upstox help center).
